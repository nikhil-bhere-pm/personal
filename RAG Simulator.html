What RAG actually does
Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with generation.

Instead of asking the LLM to answer only from what it learned during training, we first retrieve relevant external information and place it inside the model's context.

Important: RAG does not magically increase the LLM's context window. It selectively chooses useful information and puts that information into the existing context window.
